Hi! I am a Quantitative Researcher at Citadel Securities. Prior to that, I was a Research Scientist at Google DeepMind, where I contributed to the Gemini 3 Pro and Gemini 2.5 Pro models. My research areas include LLMs, agentic AI, and quantitative trading, with particular interests in their intersections.

I received my Ph.D. from Duke University in 2025, advised by Prof. Neil Gong. Earlier, I earned a B.Eng. from the University of Science and Technology of China in 2020.
